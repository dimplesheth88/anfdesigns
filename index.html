About me

Hi, I'm Dimple.

With 16 years of interior design experience, I excel in creating tailored spaces, from budget to luxury. I emphasise sustainability by incorporating eco-friendly elements and repurposing furniture. As a freelance designer, I work with each client directly, from the first call to the final styling.

My designs focus on aesthetics, and also aim to evoke cherished memories. I extend my passion for art by crafting bespoke artworks that resonate with each client's thoughts and emotions, adding a personal touch to every project.
